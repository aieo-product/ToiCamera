ToiCamera

Toi (問い) is Japanese for a question — and it sounds like toy. A wrist-worn AI camera you ask questions with: point, shoot, and it explains the world — on screen and out loud.

Build yours — Worker setup Source on GitHub

What it does

📷 Zero-lag shutter

The live finder frame is the photo — no capture delay, what you see is what you shot.

🗣 Speaks its mind

AI explanations in Animal-Crossing-style chirps, or real TTS with a voice your Worker defines.

🎤 Hold-to-talk Q&A

Hold the yellow button and ask about the photo — STT, context-aware answer, spoken back.

📍 Location-aware

GPS + reverse geocoding: town-level location, nearest station, place-flavored explanations.

🌐 Trilingual

日本語 / English / 中文 — UI and AI content switch together, right on the watch.

🧱 LEGO-compatible back

3D-printed backpack plate; camera & GPS clip anywhere, LEGO Technic pin pitch.

Your device, your backend, your model

ToiCamera ships with no shared server. You deploy a small Cloudflare Worker under your own account — it holds your API keys, defines the model menu the watch shows in Settings, picks the TTS voice, and can even route to a local LLM running on your own machine through a Cloudflare Tunnel. The firmware never hardcodes a model name.
